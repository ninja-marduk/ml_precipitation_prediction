# First full chunk of the p30 factorial: seed 42, BASIC, 2026-08-20

`factorial_p30_42_basic`. Three graph operators on the corrected pipeline at
patience 30, full 61x65 grid, writing to `V4_GNN_TAT_Models_p30`.

## Cost, measured

| variant | epochs | best | s/epoch | peak GPU | hours |

| | | | | **total** | **4.82** |

Against a projection of 9.1 hours. The projection applied GAT's rate to every
operator, and at full scale GCN runs at 0.36 and SAGE at 0.20 of it, a wider gap
than the light rehearsal suggested (0.54 and 0.45 there, where fixed overhead
dominates the epoch). Peak memory matches the earlier measurement exactly for GAT.

Reprojected with the measured per-operator rates, the five remaining chunks come to
about 28.5 hours rather than 49.5, and the whole factorial to about 33 rather than
59.

## Patience 30 changed nothing here

GAT/BASIC at seed 42 was already available at patience 15. The two runs agree:

| | patience 15 | patience 30 |
|---|---|---|
| best epoch | 3 | 3 |
| best val loss | 0.6879 | 0.6879 |
| pooled R2 | 0.3893 | 0.3893 |

Predictions are not bit-identical but differ by at most 0.09 mm and by 0.007 mm on
average, on a field averaging about 180 mm: floating-point reduction order, not a
different result. So the concern that motivated the longer patience does not apply
to this bundle. Every operator here peaked by epoch 5. It may still apply to PAFC,
where one archived seed peaked at epoch 25, and that is the chunk to watch.

## The ranking inverts

Like for like, seed 42, BASIC, archived April pipeline against the corrected one:

| variant | archived | corrected | change | rank archived | rank corrected |
|---------|---------:|----------:|-------:|--------------:|---------------:|
| GAT | 0.5963 | 0.3882 | -0.208 | 1 | 3 |
| GCN | 0.4926 | 0.4387 | -0.054 | 2 | 2 |
| SAGE | 0.3809 | 0.4480 | **+0.067** | 3 | **1** |

The movement is not a constant offset between pipelines. GAT falls by 0.21, GCN by
0.05, and SAGE rises. A ranking computed on the archived runs therefore carries no
information about the ranking on the corrected ones, which is the objection this
retrain was commissioned to settle, now measured rather than argued.

**One seed, and the seed matters.** Seed 42 is the draw the earlier investigation
identified as fortunate for GAT in the archived run and unfortunate in the corrected
one: archived peak 0.6284 sits 2.3 standard deviations above its own three-seed
mean. Across three seeds the archived-to-corrected change for GAT/BASIC is -0.035,
not -0.208. For GCN and SAGE there is no corrected multi-seed figure yet. So the
inversion is established for this seed and is a reason to finish the factorial, not
yet a result about the factorial.
