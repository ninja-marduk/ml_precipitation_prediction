1. THE 33 WINDOWS ARE NOT 33 INDEPENDENT OBSERVATIONS
==========================================================================
  33 windows x 12 leads = 396 scored (window, lead) pairs
  distinct target months: 44
  each month is scored between 1 and 12 times
  a window-level test writing N=33 therefore claims 9.0 times
  the information the record holds, before any serial correlation.

  non-overlapping 12-month windows in 44 months: 3
  independent annual cycles in 44 months: 3.7
  => the effective N for a window-level test is 3 to 4, not 33.

  Nemenyi critical difference, k=8 models, recomputed:
       N        CD   reading
  ------------------------------------------------------------
      33     1.828   as reported, treating windows as independent
       4     5.250   annual cycles in the record
       3     6.062   non-overlapping windows

  The critical difference grows by a factor of 3.3 when the
  dependence is respected. A Friedman/Nemenyi analysis at N=33 is
  anticonservative for the omnibus test and conservative for the post-hoc,
  so quoting both is quoting whichever direction suits the claim.
  With N=3 the post-hoc separates nothing: CD=6.06 exceeds the whole
  range of mean ranks that 8 models over 3 blocks can produce.

2. MINIMUM DETECTABLE EFFECT OF THE THREE-SEED PAIRED DESIGN
==========================================================================
  n=3 paired seeds, t_crit=4.303 at 2 d.f.
  a difference separates only if it exceeds t_crit x s_d / sqrt(n) = 2.484 x s_d
  observed paired s_d across the 15 comparisons: 0.028 to 0.174 (median 0.092)
  => minimum detectable effect: 0.069 to 0.431 in R2, median 0.228
  the whole factorial spans 0.109 in R2, so the median MDE is 2.1 times
  the entire range of the design. 'Not significant' here means the
  design cannot resolve differences of the size anyone would claim,
  not that the architectures are equivalent.
