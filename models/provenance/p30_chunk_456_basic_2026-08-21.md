At n=3 the bar is |t| > 4.303. Nothing comes within an order of magnitude of it.
Within this bundle the three message-passing operators are indistinguishable, and a
table built from any single run would have named a different winner depending on
which run it was.

## The cell that looked most stable is the least stable

SAGE with BASIC features scored 0.4480 and 0.4483 at the first two seeds, a spread
of 0.0003, the tightest cell in the design. The third seed gives 0.3083, and its
standard deviation becomes 0.0807, the widest of all six cells.

Two seeds are not merely weak evidence about a difference, they are misleading
evidence about stability. That bears directly on what three seeds can support, and
it is an argument for reporting dispersion rather than for trusting it.

## A reproducibility check that passed

GAT with BASIC over three seeds gives 0.4462 +/- 0.0541. The earlier corrected run
of the same cell, at patience 15 and on a different Colab account, gives
0.4462 +/- 0.0542. Same to four decimals, across a change of stopping rule and of
machine allocation.

## What remains

Seed 456 on PAFC, three runs, about 4.8 hours. The PAFC comparisons are the ones
with a chance of resolving: across the two seeds available, GAT over GCN is
+0.0550 with a paired spread of 0.0063, and GAT over SAGE is +0.0570 +/- 0.0126.
If the third seed behaves like the first two, the manuscript will be able to say
that the bundle effect and GAT's lead within PAFC are resolved while the operator
comparison within BASIC is not, which is a sharper claim than either half alone.
