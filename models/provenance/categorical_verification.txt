--- reading ---
POD is the fraction of observed tercile events the forecast caught; FAR the
fraction of forecast events that did not occur; CSI the hits over hits plus
misses plus false alarms; bias the forecast event count over the observed one.
A bias far below 1 means the forecast almost never issues the event.
The climatological mean is a single value per cell and month, so whether it
falls inside a tercile is fixed in advance and does not respond to the year
being forecast. Its scores are therefore a property of the climatology's
position in the distribution, not forecast skill, which is the point of
scoring it here: the reference that wins on R^2 cannot signal an anomaly.
