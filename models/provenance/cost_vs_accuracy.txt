  The threshold is the same one the factorial uses: with three paired
  seeds a difference separates only if it exceeds 2.484 x s_d. Every
  pair here is below it, so no accuracy difference in this family is
  resolvable, whatever it cost to obtain.

  Read the cost column against that. Attention costs 5.3 times what
  neighbourhood averaging costs and holds 1.4 times the memory, for a
  difference of +0.024 in mean R2 that the design cannot detect.

  Parameter count does not explain the cost. The three operators sit
  within 8K parameters of one another and their training times differ
  by a factor of five, because the cost of a graph model scales with
  edges traversed per timestep rather than with weights.
