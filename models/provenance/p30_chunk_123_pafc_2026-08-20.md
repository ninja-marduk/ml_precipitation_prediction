# Third p30 chunk: seed 123, PAFC, and the patience question is answered

`factorial_p30_123_pafc`. This chunk was run next on purpose: it holds the one
archived cell that peaked late, and so it decides whether the longer patience the
whole plan was built around was worth buying.

| variant | epochs | best | s/epoch | peak GPU | hours |
|---------|-------:|-----:|--------:|---------:|------:|
| GAT | 32 | **2** | 335.3 | 29.22 GB | 2.98 |
| GCN | 33 | 3 | 121.7 | 20.37 GB | 1.12 |

Nine of the eighteen cells are now done, at 14.29 hours.

## Patience 30 buys nothing, and we now know why

GAT on PAFC at seed 123 is the cell that peaked at epoch 25 in the archived run.
On the corrected pipeline it peaks at **epoch 2**.

Best epochs across all nine corrected cells: 2, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3, 3, 5, 5. Not one
needed more than five, and patience 15 would have stopped none of them early. The
late peak was a property of the archived pipeline, not of the PAFC bundle, so the
reason the plan raised the patience does not exist on the pipeline the plan runs.

This is measurable rather than inferred: GAT/BASIC at seed 42 exists at both
settings and gives the same best epoch, the same validation loss and the same
pooled R2 to four decimals. Where the optimum is early, the stopping rule is inert.

**The remaining nine runs stay at patience 30 anyway.** Dropping to 15 would save
about 6.5 hours of the roughly 15.6 remaining, and the case against is stronger than
that saving. Protocol consistency across the factorial is the entire purpose of this
retrain; changing a training knob halfway through reintroduces the defect being
fixed, and a reviewer who has already caught this benchmark mixing pipelines is
owed a factorial that mixes nothing. The extra epochs cost time and change no
result, which is a clean thing to be able to say.

## Seed 123, PAFC, like for like

| variant | archived | corrected | change |
|---------|---------:|----------:|-------:|
| GAT | 0.5566 | 0.4846 | -0.072 |
| GCN | 0.5525 | 0.4251 | -0.127 |
| SAGE | 0.5354 | 0.4366 | -0.099 |

All three fall, by between 0.07 and 0.13, and the ordering is preserved: GAT first
in both. At seed 42 the changes ranged from -0.208 to +0.067 and the ordering
reversed. So the pipeline change is not a constant offset and its effect differs by
seed as well as by cell, which is the reason the archived factorial cannot be
carried over and the reason the remaining seed matters.

GAT leads the PAFC bundle at both seeds run so far, which is the direction the
manuscript's surviving ranking claim needs.
