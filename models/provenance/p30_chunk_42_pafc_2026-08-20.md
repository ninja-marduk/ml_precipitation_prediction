## Patience 30 has not yet earned its cost

Best epochs across the six cells: 3, 5, 2, 5, 3, 2. Nothing came close to needing
thirty epochs of waiting. The archived run that motivated the longer patience is
seed 123 on GAT/PAFC, which peaked at epoch 25, and that cell has not been rerun
yet. Seed 123 is therefore the chunk that decides whether the extra patience was
worth buying.

Three things follow, and the first is the one that matters.

**GAT with BASIC features goes from first to last.** That is the configuration the
companion study reported as its headline. It was already known to be fifth of six
once seeds were accounted for within the archived pipeline; on the corrected
pipeline at this seed it is sixth of six, and the two effects are separate. One is
about which seed was quoted, the other about which pipeline produced it.

**The bundle effect is cleaner than the archived run suggested.** All three PAFC
cells now sit above all three BASIC cells. In the archived ranking they interleaved,
with GAT/BASIC first and GCN/BASIC fifth.

**The claim the manuscript currently makes survives.** It says GAT with the
precipitation-lag bundle is the strongest configuration once seeds are accounted
for. On the corrected pipeline at seed 42 it is first, having been third in the
archived ranking. The claim was reached from the archived multi-seed factorial and
is confirmed here by a different route.

## What this still is not

One seed of three. Seed 42 is the draw the earlier investigation identified as
fortunate for GAT/BASIC in the archived run and unfortunate in the corrected one, so
the -0.208 on that cell is the largest single movement in the table and the least
representative. Seeds 123 and 456 decide whether the reordering holds.
